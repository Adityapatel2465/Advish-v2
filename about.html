Our clinic

A neurosurgery clinic built around listening

Advish Brain and Spine Care Centre is on Jetalpur Road, Vadodara. The clinic focuses on brain and spine conditions and aims to make it easy for patients and families to get clear information and connect with the care team.

Every patient's concerns and questions matter. We take time to explain what is happening, what the options are, and what to expect next – so you can make decisions with confidence.

Listening first

Meet your specialist

Dr. Pratik Patel

MBBS · MS (General Surgery) · MCh (Neurosurgery) · Endovascular Neurosurgeon

Dr. Pratik Patel is a neurosurgeon and endovascular neurosurgeon caring for brain and spine conditions, with over 8 years of experience and more than 3,000 surgeries performed. Endovascular techniques let him treat many blood-vessel problems of the brain from inside the vessels, without open surgery, where suitable. He believes in explaining every option clearly before any decision is made.
