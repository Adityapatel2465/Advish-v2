Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?
Appointments are booked by phone. Call +91 96626 59406 and the team will fix a convenient time for you.
What should I bring to my first consultation?
Please bring any MRI or CT scans (films, reports and CD if available), earlier prescriptions, discharge summaries, and a list of the medicines you currently take.
Do I need a referral from another doctor?
You can contact the clinic directly. If another doctor has referred you, please bring their notes along.
What are the clinic timings?
Consultations are by appointment. Please call +91 96626 59406 to confirm current timings before your visit.
Does every back or neck problem need surgery?
Not always. Many spine problems are first managed without surgery. Dr. Patel will explain your options and why a particular treatment is recommended.
What should I do in an emergency?
For sudden weakness, facial droop, trouble speaking, a serious head injury, loss of consciousness or any other emergency, call 108 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.
